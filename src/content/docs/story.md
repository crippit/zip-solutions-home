# Dignity as Our North Star

At Zip Solutions, we believe that **dignity is the foundational metric of genuine accessibility.** 

Communication tools should never make a person feel singled out, burdened by complex IT hurdles, or locked out by steep subscription costs. When a student in a classroom, a congregant in a house of worship, or an attendee at an international conference struggles to understand spoken audio, the failure is not theirs—it is a failure of the environment.

### Why We Build for Dignity

* **User Agency & Control:** Every person has unique visual and cognitive preferences. By allowing users to receive live captions on their personal smartphones or tablets, they can independently choose their own font sizes, high-contrast themes, and languages without having to ask for special treatment or alter the public display screen.
* **Invisible, Frictionless Access:** Requiring software downloads, admin account credentials, or credit card authorizations creates immediate barriers. Our tools launch instantly inside any modern browser with zero friction.
* **Respecting Privacy as Dignity:** A person’s spoken conversations, questions, and accommodation needs are deeply personal. We never record voice audio, never build behavioral ad profiles, and store transcripts locally on the user’s device.

## How Zip Solutions Began

### 2013–2020: Educators & Builders
James and Chris met in 2013 at an educational technology workshop. Instead of merely listening to lectures, they spent their sessions collaborating on software that enabled teachers to sync dynamic schedules into Google Calendar. Over the following years, they built software tools, home automation systems, and assistive scripts, speaking at technology symposiums across North America and contributing to the Google for Education blog for Global Accessibility Awareness Day.

### 2020–2023: The Classroom Turning Point
During the 2020–21 school year, Chris began teaching a student with hearing loss and quickly discovered how fundamentally inaccessible ordinary school environments can be. From fire alarms and morning announcements to auditorium assemblies and theatrical productions, standard accommodations frequently fell short. 

Chris and James initially developed *SlidesCaptions*, a browser extension that allowed educators to isolate and expand built-in presentation captions. Recognizing that schools needed a permanent, full-featured, and privacy-respecting platform, Chris also spearheaded assistive listening installations in his high school auditorium and collaborated with educational specialist Tracey Green on classroom accessibility guidance.

### 2023–Present: From Innovation Project to Global Impact
In 2023, Chris was selected for the inaugural Dell Education Innovation Accelerator. Leveraging insights from the accelerator and the Google Certified Innovator community, **Zip Captions** was officially launched.

What started as a grassroots initiative to support students with hearing loss has grown into an international platform serving thousands of educators, community broadcasters, conferences, and individuals worldwide. 

Solutions Zip (operating as Zip Captions and Zip Solutions) is proudly based near Montreal, Quebec, Canada.
