## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. The Master Roster & Grades Hub
Stop clicking through dozens of tabs to find missing work. Classroom Sync automatically compiles your entire teaching load into two master databases:
* **Master Roster:** A flattened directory of every student, mapped dynamically to the courses they are taking.

### 2. AI-Optimized Link Formatting (DLP Bypass)
Ever tried having an AI draft an email with Google Classroom links, only to get `[[link removed]]`? Enterprise AI security filters often block raw URLs.
* **The Fix:** Classroom Sync automatically strips standard protocols and wraps your resources in natural, conversational sentences (e.g., *"Visit classroom.google.com/c/123 to find these resources."*).
* **The Result:** Flawless integration with Workspace Studio and Gemini, allowing your AI to generate perfect, clickable catch-up emails for absent students without triggering security blocks.

* **Live Status Feedback:** Real-time visual logs and indicators let you know exactly what the API is fetching.
* **Premium UI:** Designed with the ZipSolutions design system (Midnight Obsidian and Soft Alabaster) for a high-contrast, modern experience.

### 4. Set-It-And-Forget-It Automation
Your data should work for you, not the other way around. Classroom Sync features a built-in scheduling engine.
* Set your database to refresh in the background every **15, 30, 60, or 90 minutes**, or opt for a lightweight **Daily Sync at 2:00 AM**.
* Always wake up to an updated spreadsheet, ready for your morning parent-teacher emails.

# Classroom Studio Sync: Turn Google Classroom Data into an AI-Powered Teaching Assistant

Managing student data across multiple classes, tracking missing work, and drafting parent communications consumes hours of an educator’s week. **Classroom Studio Sync** bridges the gap between your learning management system and next-generation language models. 

By automating the export of live Google Classroom records into structured, AI-ready data files, Classroom Studio Sync transforms raw academic history into a personal, context-aware teaching assistant.

---

## What is Classroom Studio Sync?

Large language models (LLMs) like Google Gemini and NotebookLM are incredibly capable, but they require clean, securely accessible data to be useful in a school setting. Manual copying and pasting often leads to truncated records, broken context windows, or formatting errors.

Classroom Studio Sync runs as a seamless extension that exports live data directly via a secure sync link. The output is structured into three foundational data pillars optimized for AI context engines:

* **Master Roster:** Enrollment sheets, student metadata, and complete parent/guardian contact coordinates.
* **Master Grades:** Comprehensive, cross-class grade histories, submission states, and real-time completion tracking.
* **Subject-Specific Tabs:** Granular task parameters, localized assignment tags, and criteria specific to individual subject areas (e.g., Math, French).

---

## Core Capabilities & AI Workflows

Once Classroom Studio Sync centralizes your classroom infrastructure, it unlocks powerful integrations with tools like **Gemini Gems** and **NotebookLM** to automate your most time-consuming admin tasks.

### 1. Intelligent Missing Work Detection & Outreach
Instead of hunting through individual assignment streams to identify who hasn't submitted their work, you can point a custom Gemini Gem at your synced data files to get an instant evaluation.

The system automatically scans submission metadata across all classes to flag missing work—even if a grade placeholder hasn't been generated in the system yet. Once identified, Classroom Studio Sync allows the AI to immediately transition from analysis to action by drafting personalized student outreach.

> **Privacy-First Automation:** The system extracts student and guardian email addresses directly from the synced Master Roster, formatting them into a clean, comma-separated string ready for your email client's BCC line. This ensures rapid outreach while strictly protecting student privacy.

### 2. Early Warning "At-Risk" Tracking
A student sitting at a passing average can easily mask an impending grade drop if missing projects haven't been factored into their total yet. Classroom Studio Sync provides the full grounding data required for predictive tracking. You can instantly surface students who are slipping or warn yourself about whose averages will collapse once overdue items are formally marked as a zero.

### 3. Diagnostic Trend Analysis
By providing complete, un-truncated datasets, Classroom Studio Sync lets you discover behavioral patterns and pedagogical needs that would otherwise remain hidden in rows of numbers. 

When tasked with looking at performance consistency, the AI assistant can quickly spot complex outliers. For instance, it can easily identify a student exhibiting a sharp discrepancy between planned evaluations (e.g., scoring 98%) and unannounced pop quizzes (e.g., scoring 60%), identifying a possible need for test-anxiety support.

### 4. Direct Newsletter Generation
Transitioning from spreadsheet data to parent-facing communications is simplified. The platform lets you instantly convert ongoing unit metrics and logistics lists into clean, visually organized templates. Using inline collaborative workspaces, this text compiles into an attractive, parent-ready communication format—complete with complex formatting rules, mathematical notations, and clear highlight boxes for major milestones.

## Advanced Cross-Curricular Synthesis with NotebookLM

For multi-subject teachers, guidance counselor workflows, or school-wide tracking, dropping your Classroom Studio Sync data into a closed-source ecosystem like NotebookLM provides deep structural synthesis.

### Unified Academic Risk Profiles
Because the extension maps multi-class parameters into a unified format, NotebookLM can analyze a single student's status across completely distinct courses simultaneously. The platform evaluates the entire academic profile to generate a cohesive report, allowing you to instantly see whether a student is struggling globally or dealing with localized friction in a specific subject.

### Multi-Language Report Card Comment Generation
Drafting meaningful, data-backed report card narratives is notoriously slow. Classroom Studio Sync supplies the deep context needed to generate complete, personalized progress evaluations in seconds. 

For schools operating in bilingual or immersion environments, the system handles linguistic shifts natively. It can compile a metric-accurate Math evaluation in English and seamlessly pivot to write a culturally and grammatically precise French progress assessment for the exact same student, sourcing evidence from the respective subject tabs.
