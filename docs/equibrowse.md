# EquiBrowse by Zip Solutions

EquiBrowse is a powerful accessibility and reading comprehension extension designed to create an equitable browsing experience for all users. Whether you're a student, a professional, or someone looking to enhance your reading efficiency, EquiBrowse provides a suite of tools to help you understand, organize, and interact with web content more effectively.

## Key Features

### 🔊 Read Aloud (Text-to-Speech)
Listen to any selected text with our integrated Text-to-Speech engine. This tool helps with pronunciation, reduces eye strain, and supports multi-modal learning.

### 📖 Smart Dictionary
Instant access to word definitions. Select any word to view its meaning without leaving your current page, helping you build vocabulary and improve comprehension on the fly.

### 🖼️ Picture Dictionary
Sometimes a picture is worth a thousand words. Our Picture Dictionary provides visual symbols for selected terms, which is especially helpful for visual learners and users with language processing needs.

### 🪄 AI Text Simplification
Powered by advanced AI, this tool simplifies complex sentences and technical jargon into easy-to-understand language. It can also provide context and explanations for difficult concepts.

### 🖍️ Multi-Color Highlighting
Organize your research and reading with persistent highlights. Choose from multiple colors to categorize information. Our highlighting engine is built to work seamlessly across the web, including specialized support for Google Docs.

### 📝 Integrated Notepad
Capture your thoughts and important snippets in a built-in notepad. Your notes stay with you as you browse, making it easy to compile research or draft summaries.

### 🔍 Focus Mode
Minimize distractions with a customizable reading mask. Focus Mode dims the surrounding page and highlights only the line or section you are currently reading, which is ideal for maintaining concentration.

### 🔡 Custom Typography & Font Tools
Take control of your reading environment. Adjust font sizes instantly and toggle **OpenDyslexic**, a specialized font designed to increase readability for users with dyslexia.

## Seamless Google Docs Integration
EquiBrowse includes a specialized engine for Google Docs. Unlike standard extensions that struggle with the Google Docs canvas, EquiBrowse provides stable text extraction, precise highlighting, and full tool compatibility within your documents.

## Design for Everyone
EquiBrowse is built with a "user-first" philosophy. The interface is intuitive, non-intrusive, and can be docked to the top of your browser or used as a sidebar to fit your workflow.
