# Zip Speech Browser Extension

Zip Speech gives a voice to those without in Google Meet. Type to speak directly into the meeting with multiple accents and languages. Zip Speech is an accessibility tool designed to give a voice to those who cannot speak during Google Meet calls.

Unlike other text-to-speech tools that play audio out of your computer speakers (causing echo), Zip Speech injects the audio directly into your Google Meet microphone stream. This means other participants hear you clearly, as if you were speaking yourself, while you can still hear the meeting audio perfectly.

In the latest update we have added AAC support for those who need pre-defined phrases. The core in built phrases automatically translate when the language is switched.
